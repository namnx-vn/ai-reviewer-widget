# Phase 6.7 — Performance & Scale

Status: ✅ Complete

## Objective

Validate reviewer performance on realistic repository sizes without sacrificing correctness or determinism, and introduce optimization only when benchmark evidence justifies it.

## Benchmark Classes

- small: 3,000 LOC
- medium: 15,000 LOC
- large: 60,000 LOC

These are deterministic synthetic benchmark categories, not product limits.

## Benchmark Contract

`ReviewBenchmarkResultV1` records:

- source parse duration
- repository indexing duration
- deterministic full-review duration
- total measured full-review duration
- incremental-scope calculation duration
- deterministic incremental-review duration
- observed peak heap
- files/lines/symbols/findings/impacted-file counts
- full-vs-incremental finding equivalence

`npm run benchmark:review -- <small|medium|large>` prints versioned JSON. An optional `--baseline=<path>` compares duration and heap measurements with an explicit regression policy.

## Medium CI Measurement

The CI quality job runs the controlled medium corpus and uploads the result as `review-benchmark-medium`.

Measured on the Phase 6.7 acceptance run:

- files: 500
- LOC: 15,000
- symbols: 15,000
- parse: ~410 ms
- repository index: ~363 ms
- deterministic full review: ~18.07 s
- total measured full review: ~18.84 s
- incremental scope calculation: ~3 ms
- deterministic incremental execution: ~17.72 s
- observed peak heap: ~407 MiB
- full/incremental findings equivalent: yes

The medium-repository Phase 6 target of `< 60 seconds` passed. The near-full incremental runtime is visible benchmark evidence that most current built-ins remain safely repository-scoped; Phase 6.7 does not hide that cost behind speculative caching or worker complexity.

## Optimization Decision

No parsed-AST cache, repository-index cache, persistent cache, or worker pool was introduced in this phase. Future optimization must be justified by benchmark evidence and preserve findings exactly.
