## Detection Contract
Never treat `req.body.amount` alone as a vulnerability. Model input, validation, authorization, domain normalization/server-side calculation, transaction boundary, idempotency, and privileged state transitions. Use medium confidence when full workflow evidence is unavailable.

## Tests
Validated vs unvalidated amounts, server-computed fees, client-controlled balances/authority, idempotency keys, replay candidates, ordered workflow guards, wrappers, and false-positive regressions.

## Exit Criteria
Business rules are explicit semantic models, not broad identifier-name heuristics.
